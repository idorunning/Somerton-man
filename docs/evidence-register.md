## Classification rules

- **Verified fact:** directly supported by a reliable cited source.
- **Reported claim:** attributed to a source but not independently verified.
- **Inference:** an interpretation derived from recorded evidence.
- **Unknown:** unresolved or insufficiently sourced.

Do not promote an inference to fact merely because it appears in multiple secondary accounts.
