# RPFA Analysis -- Results Summary

## What was tested

The RPFA hypothesis proposes that the letters in the Somerton Man tamam-shud code are initials of South Australian railway stations associated with a specific location. We tested whether Marino station on Adelaide's southern coastal line is an unusually good anchor point: does a circle of radius r centred on Marino capture railway stations whose first letters match the code letters better than circles centred on other points across South Australia?

## What the missing letters tell us

- At **15 km** the letters **IQ** are not covered by any station within that radius of Marino.

## Overall verdict

At 15 km Marino ranks in the top 5% of all tested SA anchor points for code-letter coverage (88% vs grid mean 0%). **This is a noteworthy result** consistent with -- though not proof of -- the RPFA hypothesis.

*Caveat: station names and coordinates were automatically extracted from a 1938 SA Railways map using Claude vision; extraction errors and ungeocoded stations limit the strength of any conclusion.*
